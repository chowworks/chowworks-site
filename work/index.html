Workforce programs · colleges · training providers

learn/differently

Every learner takes a different path to the same goal: job-ready skills. AI adapts each learner's plan to close the gap between what they can do and what the job needs. Microlearning ends in real-world simulations scored on judgment, not recall.

For staff, the admin work is automated. Completion, credentials, and employment outcomes show up in live dashboards instead of spreadsheets.

Grant-funded nonprofits · training providers

audit/differently

Every grant and contract gets one consistent process, with its records, proof, and participant files in one secure place. Proof is attached as the money is spent, everything is cross-checked every month, and anything missing is flagged before an auditor sees it.

It works alongside your books, not instead of them, and auditors get read-only access with every action logged.

Career centers have a handful of coaches for thousands of students. InterviewDifferently gives every student a realistic place to practice: AI workplace simulations where each decision branches the scenario, scored across four competencies with written feedback.

Consumer · group travel

Travel HQ

A home base for the friend who plans the trip: one shareable trip page, who's in, and a running tab of who's paid. It tracks the money but never touches it, so there are no processor fees.

It's here because the same skills that make a grant tracker work make a group trip work: clear records, shared visibility, no chasing people.
