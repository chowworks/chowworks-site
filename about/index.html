chow/about

A collective of senior engineers in Washington, DC.

Chow.Works is a small collective of software developers led by Chris Howard. We've been taking on builds since 2021, and we pick work that helps people learn, find work, or get served well by the institutions they depend on.

Why "the people who run them"

Schools, agencies, and nonprofits are usually run by a few people doing the work of ten. The software they're handed is either built for a company a hundred times their size or held together with spreadsheets. We build the tools in between: focused, maintainable, and made for the staff who actually use them.

Chris Howard, founder

Chris has written software for 25 years. He started at NASA Goddard building data systems for satellite missions, then spent two decades building mission systems for U.S. national security agencies at L-3, Raytheon, and Clarity, where he led a first-in-agency cloud migration. Today he's a Software Development Manager at AWS, where he built a hiring and training program that brought on more than 200 engineers.

He has also taught computer science since 2015 at the University of Maryland Global Campus, and at Prince George's and Howard Community Colleges. Teaching is why so much of what we build is about learning.

More about Chris at chrishoward.fyi. The giving-back side lives at chrishoward.gives.
